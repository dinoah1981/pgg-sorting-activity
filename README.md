# PGG Unit 5: Lesson 2 — Time Period Sorting Activity

An interactive, gamified sorting activity for **Principles of Global Geography**. Students examine 13 historical source cards and sort them into the correct time period.

## Time Periods
- **Pangaea** (~335 million years ago)
- **~10,000 Years Ago** (end of Ice Age)
- **~1,000 Years Ago** (medieval era)
- **~100 Years Ago** (industrial era)
- **Today** (modern world)

## Features
- 13 source cards with real educational images from Wikimedia Commons
- Drag-and-drop or tap-to-place (Chromebook friendly)
- Point system with streak bonuses
- 3 hint tokens that glow the correct column
- Timer, sound effects, confetti on perfect score
- Full-screen modal for studying each source

## How to Use
1. Students open the page on Chromebooks
2. Tap a card to study it, then drag or tap to place it in a time period column
3. Record answers on the paper worksheet
4. Click "Check Answers" when all 13 are placed
